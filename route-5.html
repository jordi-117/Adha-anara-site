Quinchana - High Mountain Birds

Description

This trail near the hamlet ascends along old rural paths to reach high areas where the Mountain Eagle is often seen. Along the way, which runs alongside the road and through some sections of forest, it is also possible to find species associated with the Magdalena River, including the Torrent Duck. It is a short but ideal experience for those who wish to combine iconic birds with accessible Andean landscapes.

Includes

  • Binoculars
  • Breakfast
  • Snacks and hydration
  • Birdwatching guide
  • Medical insurance
  • Photos and checklist
  • Private transportation (round trip)
  • Accommodation (1 night)

Details

  • Duration: 1 day
  • Hiking distance: 4 km
  • Start time: 5:30 am
  • Difficulty: High
  • Altitude: 2,200 m.a.s.l.
  • Average number of species: 30–60

Location

Trail Through Río Blanquito, Vereda Puerto Quinchana, San Agustín, Department of Huila, Colombia

Best time

Year round

Group size

Max 6 people

What to bring

Comfortable hiking shoes, warm jacket, hat, sunscreen, mosquito repellent

Climate

Cool to cold in altitude, occasional rain, warm days most of the time

Ecosystems

Andean cloud forest, primary and secondary

Professional guide

Professional birding guide with extensive local knowledge

Types of Plant Cover

High-altitude forest and vegetation typical of cold Andean climates. Riverside vegetation along the trail beside the Río Blanquito.

Species of Interest

(Spizaetus isidori)
(Pharomachrus auriceps)
(Pyrrhura melanura)
(Diglossa caerulescens)

Tour Plan

Day 1 – Arrival

01
3:00 p.m.

Arrival at the Puerto Quinchana countryside. Transfer to accommodation and preparation for the next day's early start.

02
5:00 – 6:30 p.m.

Visit to the Eagle and Endangered Species Museum of the Department of Huila. An introduction to the key species of this ecosystem.

03
7:00 p.m.

Dinner. Rest and preparation for the early morning of Day 2.

Day 2 – Birdwatching

04
5:00 a.m.

Departure for the Crested Eagle Trail — prime time for the Black-and-chestnut Eagle and Golden-headed Quetzal.

05
6:30 a.m.

Packed breakfast on the Eagle Trail while scanning the canopy for raptors and quetzals.

06
7:00 a.m.

Continuation of birdwatching — look for Tapaculos, Antpittas, and Maroon-tailed Parakeet in the dense Andean forest.

07
10:00 a.m.

Snack break on the trail. Rest, hydrate, and review the morning checklist.

08
10:30 a.m.

Return along the trail with continued birdwatching — flycatchers and tanagers often more active during descent.

09
12:30 p.m.

Lunch. A well-deserved rest before the afternoon Torrent Duck trail session.

10
1:30 p.m.

Departure for the hamlet of Puerto Quinchana and the riverside Torrent Duck trail.

11
2:00 – 4:00 p.m.

Birdwatching on the Torrent Duck trail — a riverside habitat with unique species not found on the morning route.

12
4:30 p.m.

Tour of the murals on the hamlet facade — a cultural stop celebrating local biodiversity and community identity.

13
5:00 p.m.

Return to your destination. End of the Puerto Quinchana – High Mountain Birds experience.

Key Bird Species
